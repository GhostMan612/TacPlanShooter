# Asset Provenance

Tactical icon SVGs, operator sprites, 8-direction atlases, portraits, WAV audio, and
5-ft map textures under `assets/` are **ORIGINAL_PROCEDURAL**, generated by the
`tools/generate_*.py` scripts in this repository.

Vault theme atlases under `addons/godot4-asset-vault/themes/` are generated by
`tools/build_theme_packs.py` in the vault (`v0.2.0`) and are ORIGINAL_PROCEDURAL.
Third-party packs are listed in the vault's `manifest.json` with CC0 / MIT provenance.
