# Privacy Policy — TacPlan Shooter v1.0.0

TacPlan Shooter is **offline-first**. We collect no personal data.

- **No telemetry, analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs.**
- **No cloud account, no login, no third-party data sharing.**
- Mission progress, roster, and settings are stored locally on your device (`user://`).
- The only network feature is optional **local Wi-Fi LAN multiplayer** using UDP port **7600** between two devices on the same network. No data leaves your LAN.
- Asset provenance: `PROVENANCE.md`; open-source/CC0 material listed in the vault `manifest.json`.

Questions: repository issues on GhostMan612/TacPlanShooter.
